About Rent It SL

Sri Lanka's trusted rental marketplace — connecting owners and renters island-wide.

Who We Are

Rent It SL is an online rental marketplace that connects people who want to rent items with those who have items to rent, all across Sri Lanka. Whether you need a vehicle for a wedding, construction equipment for a project, a house for long-term stay, or party items for an event — Rent It SL is the platform for you.

We were built with a simple goal: make renting in Sri Lanka easy, transparent, and safe. Our platform is free to list on and open to everyone — from individual owners to businesses.

What We Offer

Rent It SL brings together a wide variety of rental categories in one convenient marketplace:

Vehicles

Cars, vans, motorcycles, trucks, buses, and tuk-tuks for any occasion.

Wedding & Events

Wedding cars, event transport, and ceremonial vehicles.

Construction

Heavy machinery, scaffolding, and construction equipment for hire.

Property

Houses, villas, apartments, and commercial spaces for short or long-term rental.

Electronics & Media

Cameras, lighting, audio equipment, and media production gear.

Party & Leisure

Party items, inflatables, outdoor equipment, and entertainment gear.

Our Values

  • Transparency: All listings are reviewed by our admin team before going live to ensure accuracy and legitimacy.
  • Trust: We offer a verified seller badge system — owners who submit government-issued ID documents are marked as verified, giving renters extra confidence.
  • Safety: We actively monitor listings for fraud and scams. Listings reported multiple times are automatically hidden and reviewed by our team.
  • Accessibility: Listing on Rent It SL is completely free. We believe everyone should have access to a fair rental marketplace.
